Rates

Prices for translations depend on a number of factors, including language of the original text, difficulty, text type (general or specialised), and urgency, and may vary greatly. Therefore, an individual quote will be provided for each request.

For an individual quote, please do not hesitate to get in touch via email. To let me prepare an accurate quote, it helps if you include the following:

  • the source language of your text
  • length of the text
  • text type / subject area
  • your desired delivery date

Alternatively, you are welcome to attach the text to be translated directly to your email.
